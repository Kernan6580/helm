Helm

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

The short version. Helm keeps your food log, weights, plan and coach conversations on your iPhone. There's no Helm account with your name or email. Helm has no ads, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and never sells your data. A few features need to send something off your iPhone to work, like a food search or a meal photo. This page lists each one, what's sent, and who receives it.

Who we are

Helm is made by Daniel Kernan, an independent developer in New York, USA ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy go to kernan6580@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Your profile and goal, targets, food log, water, weights you type in, workouts you record in Helm, your meal plan and grocery prices, your coach chat and the things you ask the coach to remember are stored only on your iPhone. We can't see them. They're removed when you use Settings → Delete all Helm data or delete the app.

Voice logging turns your speech into text on your iPhone with Apple's speech recognition. The audio isn't saved and isn't sent anywhere.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Helm reads your steps, active energy, workouts, sleep, weight and food from the Apple Health app, and saves the food, water, weight and workouts you log in Helm to Apple Health. You choose each kind of data in Apple's permission screen and can change it any time in the Health app or iPhone Settings.

Data from Apple Health is used only to run Helm's features for you. It's never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared except as described under "Claude" below, which happens only after you agree to it. "Delete all Helm data" can also remove everything Helm saved to Apple Health.

When something leaves your iPhone

Food search

The words you search for are sent to Helm's server, which looks them up in the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central database. The server keeps search results for up to 30 days so repeat searches are fast. Results are stored by the search words only, not by who searched.

Barcode scanning

The barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database, to find the product. Nothing else is sent.

Describing a meal and the coach on Apple Intelligence

When you describe a meal or chat with the coach, Helm normally uses Apple Intelligence. That runs on your iPhone, or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute, where Apple uses your request only to answer it and doesn't keep it. See Apple's privacy policy.

Claude (optional)

The coach can also use Claude, made by Anthropic. Helm asks you first, once, and shows exactly what will be sent. If you agree, Helm sends your message along with today's food, water and workouts, your weight trend (including weights from Apple Health), your goal and meal plan, foods you can't eat or don't like, and things you've told the coach to remember.

It goes through Helm's server, which passes it to Anthropic and doesn't store it. Anthropic uses it to answer you, doesn't use it to train its models, and may keep it for a limited time for safety and legal reasons. See Anthropic's privacy policy. You can turn Claude off any time in Coach → What your coach knows.

Meal photos (Helm Pro)

When you log a meal from a photo, Helm asks you first, once. The photo is made smaller on your iPhone and sent through Helm's server to Claude to find the foods. If Claude can't answer, Apple Intelligence tries instead. Only the photo and any note you add are sent. Helm doesn't save the photo, and Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. The nutrition always comes from the USDA database.

An anonymous ID and request counts

To protect the server from abuse, Helm creates a random anonymous ID through Google Firebase. It has no name, email or phone number attached. Apple's App Attest checks that requests come from the real Helm app. The server counts how many Claude requests and meal photos each anonymous ID makes per day, to apply daily limits, and deletes those counts after two days. Google keeps short-term server error logs, which never contain your messages, food or health data.

Helm Pro purchases

Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat keeps the record of your subscription, tied to your anonymous ID, so Helm knows whether Pro is active.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools that collect your data.
  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • We never use your health data for anything but Helm's features for you.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete all Helm data removes everything Helm keeps on your iPhone, and optionally everything it saved to Apple Health.
  • Apple Health: change what Helm can read or save in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps → Helm.
  • Claude: turn it off in Coach → What your coach knows.
  • Ask us: email kernan6580@gmail.com with any privacy question or request. Because there's no account with your name or email, we hold almost nothing that can identify you, but we'll help with whatever we do hold.

Health information

Helm gives general guidance on food, weight and exercise. It isn't medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to your doctor before making big changes, especially if you have a health condition or are pregnant or breastfeeding.

Children

Helm is for people 13 and older. It doesn't set calorie targets for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you think a child has used Helm, contact us and we'll help remove anything we hold.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your iPhone, Helm will tell you in the app before it happens.
